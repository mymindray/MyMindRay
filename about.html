Why we built My MindRay

Student life moves fast, and it's easy for stress, poor sleep and low mood to build up quietly between exams, deadlines and everything else. My MindRay was built with one goal: give students a single, calm place to notice how they're really doing before it becomes a bigger problem — and an easy path from that noticing to real support.

What's inside

Dashboard. One screen showing your wellness streak, check-in streak, wellness goals progress, WHO-5 well-being graph, and quick access to everything else.

Daily check-in. A 30-second mood, sleep and energy log with space for a short note, so patterns become visible over days and weeks.

WHO-5 well-being score. Your dashboard's emotional graph is based on the WHO-5 Well-Being Index, a short, internationally used self-report scale (0–100, higher is better) that research links to general mental wellbeing. It is a screening tool, not a diagnosis.

Relax practices. Short, guided breathing and grounding exercises paired with calming visuals, for the moments you need two quiet minutes.

Journal. A private, searchable space to write freely — entries can be edited or deleted any time.

AI desk. A supportive assistant that reflects on your check-ins and can point you toward a relevant practice or reading — never a replacement for a real doctor.

Educational library. Short, practical material on stress, sleep and self-kindness, written for students rather than clinicians.

Doctors directory. Browse doctors by specialty, see their free times across the day, and schedule a consultation when you're ready to talk to someone.

Calendar, notifications, and care tools. Keep upcoming consultations organised, get gentle early-care nudges when your data suggests it could help to talk to someone, and see how each part of the dashboard connects to the next.

Our approach to safety

My MindRay is a self-reflection and awareness tool, not a hospital, a therapist, or an emergency service. When check-ins stay low or stressed for several days, or a WHO-5 score trends low over about a week of use, the dashboard gently suggests talking to a parent, a friend, or a doctor — because the goal was never to keep a student's wellbeing inside an app, it's to help them reach out to a real person sooner. If you or someone you know is ever in immediate danger, call 112 (India), or the KIRAN mental health helpline at 1800-599-0019 (toll-free, 24/7).

Data and privacy, briefly

We only collect what's needed to run the app — your account details and your own wellness activity — and we never sell your data. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

About this project

My MindRay is an independent wellness platform designed and developed to give students a reliable, private space to track their mental wellbeing. Sample names shown in demo mode (such as doctor profiles) are fictional and used for illustration only.

What's next

We're actively working on syncing your dashboard, journal and activity to your account so it's available from any device you log in from, alongside improvements to doctor scheduling and notifications.
