What My MindRay is

My MindRay is a wellness dashboard designed for students. It brings your daily check-ins, WHO-5 well-being score, wellness streak, relax practices, journal, educational library and upcoming doctor consultations into one calm screen.

Getting started

Create an account with an email and password, or continue with Google, from the login page. Prefer to look around first? Use the Demo button on the home page — it loads the entire site with sample data so nothing you tap affects a real account. Your real dashboard only appears once you log in and open it from "Open dashboard" or the sidebar.

Daily check-in and WHO-5

The daily check-in asks for your mood (calm, okay, low or stressed), how you slept, your energy, and an optional short note. Your Emotional State chart uses the WHO-5 Well-Being Index, a widely used 0–100 wellness scale where higher is always better. It combines your recent check-ins with exercise and journal activity, and needs about a week of check-ins before it is a reliable read on a longer trend.

Wellness and check-in streaks

Your check-in streak grows any day you check in. Your wellness streak grows on days you mark calm or okay, and resets when you mark low or stressed — it is meant to reflect steady wellbeing, not just app usage.

Relax practices

The Relax page has short guided breathing and grounding practices, each paired with a calming visual (rain, ripple, pebble, or butterfly). Marking a practice as finished updates your wellness goals progress and your WHO-5 score for the day.

Journal

Journal is a private space to write freely. Entries can be edited, deleted, and searched by date or by text — use the search box above your entries to find an older page quickly.

Educational library

Short, practical reads on topics like breathing and meditation techniques, spotting sources of stress, resetting sleep before exams, and speaking to yourself with more kindness. Mark any source as done to count it toward your progress.

Doctors and consultations

The Doctors page lists profiles with their specialty and free times across the day, so you can see when a doctor is open versus already booked before scheduling a consultation. This schedules a consultation slot — it does not start an in-app chat with a doctor.

Notifications

Notifications cover two kinds of alerts: upcoming consultation reminders, and early care signals — a gentle nudge shown when your WHO-5 score or recent mood suggests it may help to talk to someone. You can turn on browser notifications from the bell icon so you still get alerts even if the tab isn't open.

Demo mode vs. your real dashboard

Demo mode fills every page — dashboard, journal, doctors, profile and more — with realistic, randomly generated sample data so you can explore safely. It never touches or overwrites your real account. Your real dashboard, reached from the navbar or "Open dashboard," always shows your actual data and starts empty until you use it.

Troubleshooting

I can't log in after signing up. Most email providers require you to confirm your address first — check your inbox (and spam folder) for a confirmation link. On the login page, a "Resend confirmation email" option appears automatically if this looks like what's happening.

My password won't save. Passwords need at least 8 characters with one letter and one number; symbols are fine, but spaces and currency symbols (€, ¥, £, ₹, etc.) are not allowed.

My data disappeared. Most activity is currently saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your browser's site data, or opening the site in a different browser or device, will show a fresh start until full account sync is available — see our Privacy Policy for details.

Something looks broken. Please tell us on the Report an issue page so we can fix it.

If you need a person right now

My MindRay is not an emergency service. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call 112 (India). You can also reach the KIRAN mental health helpline at 1800-599-0019 (toll-free, 24/7) or iCall at 9152987821.
