What My MindRay is

My MindRay keeps mood check-ins, wellness goals, sessions, exercises, and upcoming visits in one calm place. It is a support tool, not a hospital and not a replacement for a counsellor.

Why choose it

Your account stores only your own progress. The AI desk can save a mood to your graph. Try the demo to look around, or sign in so your dashboard follows you on any device.
