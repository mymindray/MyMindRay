2. What My MindRay is

My MindRay is a wellness dashboard designed for students. It helps students track mood and sleep with short daily check-ins, see a WHO-5 style well-being score, complete guided breathing and relaxation practices, keep a private journal, read short educational material on stress and sleep, and browse a directory of doctors to schedule a consultation.

3. What My MindRay is not

My MindRay is not a hospital, clinic, telemedicine platform, or licensed healthcare provider. It does not diagnose or treat any medical or mental health condition, and nothing on the site should be read as medical advice. Doctor profiles are for browsing and scheduling a consultation only — they do not create a doctor-patient relationship until you actually meet or speak with that doctor through their own practice. My MindRay is also not an emergency response service: it cannot see your messages in real time or dispatch help.
